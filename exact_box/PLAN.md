## 1. Question
With the bootstrap box replaced by a box whose coverage is exact at any sample size, does the global no-drop test (a) keep its level when the true drop sits just under the 0.25 margin, and (b) keep useful power in 2D at walker budgets people use?

## 2. Why the level is exact (to be checked in code before the run, item 6.1)
Walkers start uniformly and are independent, so the return count at grid time t_j is Binomial(W, q(t_j)), W = walkers per node x N. A two-sided Clopper-Pearson interval at level alpha/K at each of the K grid times (alpha = 0.05, K = the FULL grid of the cell, fixed before data) covers all K values of q at once with probability at least 0.95, whatever the dependence across times. On that event the true return curve is an admissible measure in the box, so a DROP declaration is false with probability at most 0.05. This holds when the window length Jw is chosen from the data, because the box covers the full grid. Conditions: uniform start, independent walkers, lazy walk (spectrum in [0, 1]), finite connected graph (the 1/N atom). The level is per dataset; it is not a false-discovery rate.

## 4. Arms
- **(a) Same-seed rerun.** C768xC16 and C160xC8^2, walkers per node 8, 32, 128, first 100 datasets each; controls ring 1024, torus2 96, torus3 24 at 32 per node, 100 each. Four procedures (convex-secant, pointwise endpoint, joint, global) under the CP box; the stored nominal-box outcome is joined per dataset.
- **(b) Near-null controls.** As P4: C_L x C_w products with exact in-window drop near 0.15, 0.20, 0.23, 0.25, 0.27, 0.30, 0.35, at least two graphs each side of 0.25; the graph list and exact drops are computed, frozen and committed BEFORE any walk. 100 datasets per graph at 32 and at 128 per node, under both boxes.
- **(c) Power map.** Families aniso2_w16 and aniso3_w8; the four largest sizes of each present in `run_p22i.core_specs()` (the list is printed into the runner header and committed before launch) x 8, 32, 128, 512 walkers per node x 100 datasets. Global test only.
- **(d) Zero-noise limit.** Each cell of (c) with the box exact q(1 +- 1e-4) (the narrowest the 800-atom grid supports; the ladder 1e-3, 1e-2 is also reported). Separates "more walkers needed" from "more grid times needed".
- **(e) Certificates.** Every CP-box DROP in (a) goes through the continuum certificate of `rt_certify.py` (column generation, interval branch and bound, verified dual); a seeded sample of 10 is re-verified in ball arithmetic (`rt_certify_arbfull.py`).
- **(f) Secondary, exploratory: exact low-order constraints.** q(1), q(2), q(3) fixed to their exact values from degrees, edges and triangles, added as equalities to the global test in arm (a) at 32 per node. Prior result: exact q(1), q(2) did not help the JOINT test (0/100 to 0/100, `RESULTS_REDTEAM_GEMINI.md`).

## 5. Quantities
Declarations (DROP / WITNESS / INCONCLUSIVE) per cell with Clopper-Pearson 95% intervals; false declarations = DROP on a graph whose exact in-window drop is below 0.25 - 0.005; CP-box half-width over nominal half-width per grid time; certified / not certified / solver failure; wall time per dataset.

## 6. Bars (fixed now; no re-tuning)
1. **Harness check (gate before any arm).** Read `Graph.sample_blocks`, `_direct` and `_steps` and confirm each grid-time count is a sum of W independent Bernoulli(q(t)) with uniform start; then on ring 1024 at 32 per node, 2,000 fresh datasets: the CP box contains the exact q at all K times in at least 95% (one-sided CP lower limit of the observed rate must not fall below 0.94). If this fails the fault is in the harness, not the theorem: stop and fix before any other arm.
2. **Level (must hold).** False declarations pooled over the controls of (a) and the sub-margin graphs of (b), CP box: observed rate at most 5%. A rate whose CP lower limit exceeds 5% contradicts section 2 and is reported as a harness or implementation fault, not as a finding.
3. **Power (GO bar).** 2D, C768xC16, CP box, global test: at least 30/100 DROP at 32 or 128 walkers per node.
4. **Power map.** In each cell of (c) the declaration count does not fall as walkers rise, within CP intervals; zero-noise cells of (d) that are DROP reach at least 80/100 by 512 per node.

## 7. Predictions (recorded so they can be wrong)
2D C768xC16 at 32 per node: 8 to 20 of 100 under the CP box (P2 predicted fewer than half of the 37 survive); at 128: at least 50. 3D: at least 95 at 32. Near-null: zero DROP at exact drops up to 0.23; non-zero power only from 0.30. Arm (f): at most 3 more declarations of 100.

## 8. Falsifiers and go/no-go
- **GO** (the paper may say the declaration level is exact, and BE/BF/BH/BJ/BM may build on the box): bars 1-3 hold.
- **LIMITED**: bar 3 fails at 32 and 128 but 512 gives at least 30/100. Report the budget; the abstract keeps a qualifier.
- **NO-GO for the wording change**: under 15/100 at every budget up to 512 while arm (d) says DROP. Then the registered fallback is a weighted Bonferroni split fixed from the exact curve of the next smaller size, written as an amendment and committed before it is run, and labelled post hoc.
- **Dead**: bar 2 fails after bar 1 passed and the code is audited clean. That would mean section 2 is wrong; the paper's present "nominal" wording stays and the error is logged in ERRATA.md.

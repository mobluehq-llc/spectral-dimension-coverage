### P7 (pick 1). Continuum certificate of the 37 two-dimensional declarations (delta = 0.25, as stored)
Object: the 37 C768xC16 datasets with stored global outcome DROP (`results/nodrop_data.csv`, rows regenerated from seeds; the regenerated outcome must equal the stored one, else the dataset is reported as "regeneration mismatch" and excluded from the count, not from the list). A declaration is CERTIFIED iff every one of its r-cells has a verified Farkas certificate over the WHOLE support s in [0, inf] (the lambda in [0, 1] atom set, the s = 0 atom with its 1/N floor, and the lambda = 0 atom s = inf).
Cells: the leaves of the `find_nodrop` r-branch-and-bound on the 799-atom grid (each OUTER-excluded), plus two coverage pads that make the cell set cover every continuum measure, not only grid measures: [d0min_grid - 0.005, d0min_grid] (normal OUTER rows, threshold from its ra) and [0, d0min_grid - 0.005] (rows: box + d0 <= d0min_grid - 0.005 only), and likewise [d0max_grid, d0max_grid + 0.005] and [d0max_grid + 0.005, inf) (box + d0 >= ra, no-drop rows at threshold ra - 0.25 - 0.005). Pad cells that do not certify make the declaration NOT certified (reason reported as "coverage").
Certificate per cell: elastic phase-1 LP (rows relaxed by slacks e >= 0, minimise sum e, HiGHS) on the current atom set gives u (row multipliers in [0, 1]), v1, v2 = -Phi with Phi > 1e-8 the infeasibility. g(s) = v1 + sum_j (P_j + Q_j s) exp(-t_j s) (analytic from u, ra, rb, threshold, 1/ref scalings), plus the s = 0 atom term -u_N, the tau column condition, and the s = inf condition v1 >= 0. Continuum check: interval branch-and-bound of g over s in (0, 1] with a second-order centred enclosure (value, first derivative at the midpoint, second derivative bound by interval evaluation), tail cell s >= 1 bounded by |term_j| <= (|P_j| + |Q_j|) exp(-t_j); shift v1 -> v1 + eps, v2 -> v2 + eps with eps the smallest value making every cell nonnegative and the tau column nonnegative; CERTIFIED cell iff v2 + eps < 0 (margin Phi - eps reported). If the check fails, the minimiser s* is added to the atom set and the LP is re-solved (exchange step, at most 12 rounds). A cell whose elastic LP has Phi <= 1e-8 or returns a verified feasible measure is a WITNESS (not certified). Solver failure (non-optimal status twice with two HiGHS methods, or round cap) is counted SEPARATELY from "not certified". Float evaluation uses 1e-12 relative outward inflation; on a seeded sample of 10 of the 37 declarations (rng seed 2211, ids in increasing order) every certified cell is re-verified with mpmath interval arithmetic (iv, 100-bit) running the same B&B and the shift computation, reported as "mp-verified" (sentinel: all 10 must agree).

S2 the 58 WITNESS datasets: the cell around the stored witness r (r +- 0.001) must NOT certify (0 certified allowed; any certified is a harness defect, rule 11, and stops the report). S3 controls: ring 1024 first 10 datasets, the same witness-cell test, none may certify. Bars: none gated beyond sentinels; a report. Prediction: at least 30 of 37 certified (grid dense and e^-24 terms negligible); the most likely failure is the coverage pads or the s in (0, 0.02/tmax) region, not the dense middle.
Reporting: certified / not certified (with reason: witness cell, coverage pad, margin, exchange cap) / solver failure, as three separate counts out of 37, plus per dataset: cells, rounds, atoms added, min margin Phi - eps, wall time.

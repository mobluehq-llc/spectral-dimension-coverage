## R1. MonoLP with exact structural constraints (Gemini 3, "ExactMonoLP")
Data: the Amendment 8/9a core cells (37 cells, 200 datasets, 32 walkers per node), regenerated from the frozen seeds (`SeedSequence([22, id, ds])`, walkers child [1], box bootstrap child [7], exactly as `run_p22i.run_dataset` group a), under the pinned environment (numpy 2.5.5). Regeneration under 2.5.5 can differ from the stored 2.4.6 walks (SM Sec. S4), so the comparison is PAIRED inside this run: baseline B0 (frozen `A8.e7_gated`, `A9.e7j_decide`) and the constrained variants are all computed on the same regenerated box. The B0 decisions are also compared with the stored `raw_amend9_a.csv` and the agreement count is reported.
Variants (measure over s in [0, inf], total mass 1 and atom at s=0 of mass >= 1/N as in B0; atoms extended by a fixed block of 60 log-spaced atoms on s in (1, 30] so that lambda = e^-s below e^-1 is representable; these atoms are invisible (< e^-24) to every grid time t >= 24):
- **C1:** B0 plus sum_k w_k e^{-s_k} = q(1) = 1/2 (lazy walk on a loop-free graph: every diagonal entry of S is 1/2).
- **C2:** C1 plus sum_k w_k e^{-2 s_k} = q(2) = tr(S^2)/N computed exactly from the graph (the graph is known to the benchmark; a user needs the degree sequence).
Equalities are imposed as two inequalities with absolute slack 1e-9 in the Charnes-Cooper variables. Also reported: B0 on the extended atom set without the constraints (control for the atom extension), on the 2D crossover cells only.
Quantities per cell: (i) endpoint-test power (decl_neg of E7: upper band at some window time below lower band at t0 minus 0.25) and joint-test power (`e7j_decide` drop); (ii) mean band width of the endpoint band over the window; (iii) simultaneous coverage of the exact d(t) over the window (endpoint band); (iv) false declarations on controls (joint, any sign); (v) infeasible datasets (no measure fits).
**Bar (report the change; "helps" is defined now):** primary outcome = change in joint-test power at 2D L=768, 32 walkers per node (baseline stored 0/200), with coverage. C1 or C2 "helps materially" iff (power at 2D L=768 rises by >= 0.20 absolute, paired) OR (median endpoint-band width over the 2D crossover cells with L >= 192 falls by >= 15%), AND pooled control coverage stays >= 93% AND no false joint declaration on controls beyond the baseline count. Otherwise "does not help".
**Prediction (recorded before data, not gated):** no material effect. Reason: the constraints fix low moments of lambda, but the data at t >= 24 constrain only the mass of eigenvalues above about 0.9, a tiny fraction of the total; the rest of the mass can move freely to satisfy q(1) = 1/2 without changing any return at t >= 24. Expected: width change < 3%, 2D L=768 joint power change 0, coverage unchanged. A material effect would contradict this and be reported as such.

## R2. Robust bias-corrected (Calonico-Cattaneo-Farrell style) local-polynomial band (Gemini 2/missing baseline)
Data: the Amendment 10c fixed-window design (18 realised crossover cells; walkers at 8, 32, 128 per node; ids and J_fixed from `cells_meta_amend10c.csv`; seeds `SeedSequence([22, id, 3000+ds])`, walker child [1]; the arms' bootstrap stream replicated by the same spawn sequence as `run_amend1011.run_10c`: one discarded spawn of 9 for mode "orig", then the 9 children of mode "fixed", stream [6]). 200 datasets per cell and budget. A regeneration check comes first: the frozen `est_E1b` is re-run on the regenerated data and must reproduce the stored `cov_simult`, `h` and `hw` of `archive/data/raw_amend10c.csv` (E1q, mode fixed) on at least 95% of datasets of a 3-cell pilot, else the arm is reported as "not reproduced" and the stored LocQuad numbers are used for comparison only.
Estimator **RBC** (no pinned Python implementation of nprobust exists in this repository; `nprobust` is R and is not available here; so it is written here and unit-tested): same half-A/half-B protocol, window (fixed J) and Gaussian kernel with return-count weights as LocQuad; bandwidth h = LocQuad's half-A cross-validated choice (quadratic CV, grid {0.7,...,4.0}); point estimate = slope of the local CUBIC at h on half B (the CCF bias-corrected estimator with rho = 1: local-quadratic estimate plus the local-cubic estimate of its leading bias equals the local-cubic estimate); standard error = bootstrap SE of that cubic slope over 200 half-B block resamples (the "inflated" SE: it contains the variance of the bias estimate); simultaneous 95% max-t band as in `score`. Unit test (`rt_test_rbc.py`): the weighted local-cubic slope recovers the exact derivative of any cubic polynomial to 1e-9 under arbitrary weights; for f(x) = exp(x) the local-quadratic slope has the known h^2 f'''/... leading bias and the local-cubic slope's error is smaller by a factor >= 5 at h = 0.7; and the RBC column of a deterministic input reproduces a hand computation.
Outputs per budget: simultaneous coverage of exact d (answered / 3600 strict), pointwise coverage, mean half-width, beside LocQuad recomputed on the same data. **Bar (descriptive):** RBC "removes the decline" iff coverage at 128 walkers per node is >= 90% and the coverage sequence over 8, 32, 128 does not fall by more than 5 points. **Prediction:** RBC raises coverage at every budget but still declines with budget (cubic bias at h >= 0.7 across the bend is not removed on a 6-18 point grid with 0.35 spacing); coverage at 128 below 90%.

## Pick 1. Continuum certificate of the 37 two-dimensional declarations
Method as pre-registered: per cell an elastic phase-1 LP gives (u, v1, v2 = -Phi); g(s) = v1 + sum_j (P_j + Q_j s) e^{-t_j s} is bounded over s in (0, 1] by interval branch-and-bound (second-order centred enclosure, 1e-12 relative inflation), tail s >= 1 by (|P_j| + |Q_j|) e^{-t_j}, plus the s = 0 atom with its 1/N floor, the s = infinity atom and the tau column; shift (eps1, eps2) and require v2 + eps2 < 0; violators are added as atoms and the LP re-solved (exchange, at most 12 rounds). Cells: leaves of the grid r-B&B plus four coverage pads (d0 below and above the grid band, continuum measures not on the grid).
**Result: 37 of 37 CERTIFIED; not certified 0; solver failures 0.** 485 cells in total (10 to 29 per dataset, mean 13.1); every cell certified, including all 148 pad cells. Exchange was needed: 16 of 37 datasets added atoms (619 atoms in total, up to 148 for one dataset; mean 16.7); the other 21 certified on the 799-atom grid alone. The one declaration whose frozen E7 gate returned LP status 4 (ds 71) certifies too. Mean 6.5 s per dataset, 242 s in all.
**mp-verified (python-flint arb balls, 100 bit, same B&B and shift, rigorous enclosures):** 10 of 10 seeded sample (rng 2211; ds 0, 1, 5, 14, 44, 54, 56, 60, 61, 89), every cell. The other 27 rest on the float branch-and-bound with 1e-12 inflation only (not directed rounding).
**Sentinels (all passed):** S1 exact q, C768 x C16, eps 1e-3, delta 0.25: CERTIFIED (10/10 cells). S2: 58/58 stored WITNESS datasets, the cell r +- 0.001 around the stored witness: all 58 returned a feasible measure (witness), 0 certified. S3: ring 1024, first 10 datasets: 10/10 witness cells, 0 certified. Falsifier `rt_certify_falsifier.py` (exact boxes, the cell around the true d0): delta ABOVE the true drop (1.104 vs 1.004; 2.12 vs 2.02) -> witness (not certified); delta below (0.804; 1.82) -> certified. A deliberately damaged certificate (eps1 cut to 0.005, or eps1 = 0 with P perturbed) fails the arb check ("unres" / "atom"); eps1 = 0.0111 vs a measured dense minimum g = -0.0109 passes, 0.005 does not. Weak-threshold cell (delta + 5): witness.
**Prediction (at least 30 of 37): right (37).** The expected failure mode (coverage pads, small-s region) did not occur.
Reading and limits (stated plainly): (i) this certifies the SAME box over the whole continuum s in [0, infinity] (the P2 Step 1 question). It does not use a new finite-sample box (P2 Step 2 not run), so it says nothing about coverage. (ii) The reported margin fraction (0.90 for every cell) is an artefact of the pruning threshold 0.1 Phi, not a measure of slack; in the one cell checked densely Phi was 4.17 and the true minimum of g was -0.011. (iii) Float rather than directed-rounding B&B on 27 of 37. (iv) The grid-only continuum gap that I expected to be the hard part (atoms between grid points, below 0.02/tmax) was closed by the exchange step in 16 datasets and by nothing in 21, so the 37 declarations were not artefacts of the 799-atom grid.
